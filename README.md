# HD Background Wallpaper App (Wallpaper Haven)

![Flutter Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/Flutter-3.11.0-blue.svg)
![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/Version-2.1.0+3-green.svg)

Welcome to **BG Wallpaper App** (also known as Wallpaper Haven), the ultimate destination for all your wallpaper needs! Whether you're looking to freshen up your device's home screen, lock screen, or both, we've got you covered with a stunning collection of high-quality wallpapers. Personalize your device to reflect your style and mood.

## 🌟 Features

*   **Vast Collection (900+ Offline Wallpapers)**: Explore various wallpapers across 9 curated categories, including nature, abstract, landscapes, animals, technology, and more. Our extensive collection ensures you'll find the perfect wallpaper for any occasion, even without an internet connection.
*   **Cloud 4K Feed**: Access a dynamic feed of the latest and greatest 4K wallpapers from the cloud.
*   **High Quality**: All wallpapers in our app are carefully curated to provide you with the highest-quality visuals (4K & HD). Say goodbye to pixelated and blurry wallpapers – only crisp and clear images here.
*   **Custom Collections**: Create and manage your own custom collections of favorite wallpapers.
*   **Live Lock/Home Mockup Preview**: Preview how a wallpaper will look on your lock screen and home screen before applying it.
*   **User-friendly Interface**: Our app is designed with simplicity in mind. Intuitive navigation and smooth browsing make finding and setting wallpapers a breeze.

## 🚀 Installation

1.  **Clone the repository:**
    ```bash
    git clone https://github.com/yourusername/hd_background_wallpaper.git
    cd hd_background_wallpaper
    ```

2.  **Install dependencies:**
    ```bash
    flutter pub get
    ```

3.  **Run the app:**
    ```bash
    flutter run
    ```
    Alternatively, build an APK for Android:
    ```bash
    flutter build apk
    ```

## 📖 Usage

1. Open the app and explore the wide range of wallpapers available.
2. Tap on a wallpaper to view it in full detail.
3. Use the Mockup Preview to see how it looks on your device.
4. Choose the "Set as Wallpaper" option to apply the selected wallpaper to your device's screen.
